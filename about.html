Two Locations · One Family-Owned Team

Two boathouses. One crew.

For generations, the Texoma Boat House family has guided North Texas and Southern Oklahoma boaters onto the water with unmatched sales, premium gear, and master craftsmanship.

Established on Lake Texoma

Family owned & operated since day one

Founded with a raw passion for watersports and lake living, Texoma Boat House began as a single service slip in the heart of Calera, OK. Through an unyielding commitment to our riders, we expanded to Dallas to create a unified regional hub.

Today, our two locations operate as a singular, highly specialized boat dealership and service station. From buying your first wakesurf boat to winterizing it, our family takes care of yours every step of the way.

Why Purchase From Your Local Dealer?

Why purchase from your local dealer?

01

Convenience

Close to home, your local dealer provides a wealth of knowledge regarding the product and on-site inventory for you to tour and test drive before making your purchase.

02

Relationships

Purchasing locally allows you to establish a relationship with your dealer representative who can provide you with convenient service and support when you need it.

03

Service after the Sale

Many dealers prioritize their purchasing customers when it comes to getting your coach serviced. You will experience the value your local dealer can add to your purchase.

04

Local Economy

Local purchases help to keep tax dollars and people at work in your community.
