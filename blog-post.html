Essential Spring Boat Maintenance Checklist

Maintenance

By Jake Morrison · Published March 15, 2026 · 6 min read

Unpacking your boat for the spring season is more than just washing away winter dust. Before you drop your hull into the lake, a thorough, methodical inspection of your boat's vital systems is the absolute difference between a perfect first run and an expensive tow back to the slip. We map out the exact boat configurations and system checks that ensure a smooth transition from dry dock to high water.

First, start with the engine and fluid compartments. Inspect all hoses for cracks, soft spots, or signs of wear under pressure. Over the winter, cold temperatures can cause rubber to contract and degrade, leading to potential failure points. Ensure all hose clamps are tightened securely. Check your engine oil levels, transmission fluid, and V-drive lubricant. If you didn't change these during winterization, now is the time to run a fresh cycle.

“There is no worse feeling than launching your wake boat for the first run of the season, only to hear a dead click of a neglected battery or feel the engine overheat from a worn-out impeller.”

— Captain Jake Morrison

Next, examine the raw water impeller. Even if it was replaced last season, sitting compressed inside the housing for months can cause the rubber fins to take a set or develop micro-tears. A damaged impeller can disintegrate within minutes of startup, clogging your cooling lines and overheating your block. Replacing it proactively is a fifteen-minute task that saves thousands in engine repairs.

Finally, test your electrical systems and safety equipment. Recharge your marine batteries fully and verify they hold a load. Clean any corrosion off the terminals and apply dielectric grease to secure the connection. Run through your auxiliary systems: test the bilge pump, check the ballast pumps for proper priming, and guarantee your navigation lights are functioning. Pack a coast-guard-approved set of life jackets, fire extinguishers, and sound signals. Now, you're ready to chase the wake.
