Rider Clinic

Spring Wake Clinic with James Sasser

April 18, 2026 • Highport Marina, Lake Texoma

About The Event

Prepare to take your wakesurfing to the absolute next level. The Boathouse Co. is proud to present an exclusive, one-day intensive riding clinic featuring 1-on-1 on-water coaching from professional wakesurfer James Sasser. Whether you are struggling to drop the rope, aiming to cross the wake consistently, or looking to master advanced 360-spin tricks, this clinic is custom-tailored to fast-track your progression in a safe, structured, and high-energy environment.

Riders will be grouped by experience level and paired with state-of-the-art Supra and Moomba towboats, equipped with precise AutoWake systems to ensure perfect, customizable wave profiles. Your registration includes professional video analysis of your runs, premium board rentals from our Pro Shop, certified life jacket fitting, and a complimentary lakeside BBQ lunch to fuel the day's progression.
