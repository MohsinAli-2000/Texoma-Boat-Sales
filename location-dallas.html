Our Legacy & Presence

More Than a Marina. A Premium Boating Destination.

Originally founded as Dallas Boathouse, this location has proudly served the DFW metroplex boating community for over a decade. Now unified as a central hub of The Boathouse Co., it continues as a premier full-service marine destination, situated just minutes off I-35E near Lewisville Lake. Our facilities feature an expansive indoor showroom, a fully stocked pro shop, and top-tier service bays.

We combine high-performance brands like Supra, Moomba, and Thor with factory-certified technicians and watersports schools to offer a truly seamless end-to-end boating lifestyle.
