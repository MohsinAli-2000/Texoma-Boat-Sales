The Legend of Supra

Passion for the Perfect Wave

Supra has been pushing the boundaries of watersports innovation since 1980, recognized globally for delivering the most refined, clean, and highly customizable surf wakes in the industry. Every boat is built with a fanatical attention to detail, combining high-performance engineering with absolute interior luxury.

As North Texas & Southern Oklahoma's premier marine dealer, Texoma Boat House is proud to stand as an authorized Supra partner. We provide complete factory-backed sales, expert customization, precision servicing, and on-water instruction to ensure your Supra always performs at its peak.

Swell Surf System

Our proprietary wave technology is completely integrated, enabling rapid shaping of tall, long, and clean waves on demand for both surf and skim styles.

Intelligent Hull Design

The hull is engineered to ride deeper in the water while keeping a remarkably smooth, dry ride for all passengers, even in heavy chop.

Premium Craftsmanship
